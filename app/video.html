🎥 Video Badge Scan

← Badge scanner

Film your badges and the computer makes the list: which ones are new and which are already in the vault.

  • Go slowly, one shelf or row at a time.
  • Stop for a second on each badge.
  • Good light, and get close enough to read the letters.
  • Shorter videos (under 3 minutes) work best. Do one wall per video.
